# HAG ICT B.V. — Product Context

## Product Purpose
HAG ICT B.V. is an Utrecht-based AI consultancy and software development firm. This website is their primary marketing presence: a single-page landing site that converts business decision-makers into leads by demonstrating AI expertise and trustworthiness.

## Register
brand

## Users
Dutch business owners, operations directors, and CTOs at SMEs in and around Utrecht. They are not deeply technical but are AI-curious. They've heard about AI, feel pressure to adopt it, and are looking for a local, credible partner who speaks plainly. They distrust hype and buzzwords. They want to see competence, not just capability claims.

## Brand
- Company: HAG ICT B.V.
- Logo: `</>` + "HAG ICT" (monospace code aesthetic)
- Tone: Direct, confident, no-nonsense Dutch pragmatism. Expertise without arrogance. Local roots, modern edge.
- Anti-hype: the site explicitly says "Geen hype, wel meetbaar resultaat." ("No hype, measurable results.")
- The code/terminal aesthetic is intentional and meaningful — they build software, they live in the command line.

## Sections
1. Nav — sticky, desktop links + mobile burger
2. Hero — typewriter headline "Meer uit uw bedrijf halen met AI", badge, subtitle, two CTAs
3. Services — AI integration as core specialisation (6 sub-points) + software dev as supporting
4. AiCapabilities — spectrum from Automatisering → Intelligentie → Autonomie (6 cards)
5. Process — 4-step flow: Kennismaking → Analyse → Bouwen → Oplevering
6. About — company story, 3 stat cards, callout quote
7. Contact — email, phone (pending), location + company card with KVK/BTW
8. Footer — typed logo animation, nav links, contact

## Strategic Principles
- Demonstrate technical credibility through the design itself (code motifs, precision)
- Local trust signal: Utrecht, real KVK number
- Conversion: every section funnels toward the contact form
- No stock photos, no generic SaaS visuals

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS landing pages (cream/white, rounded everything, floating laptop mockups)
- Neon-on-black "hackerman" aesthetics
- Overly corporate navy-and-gold "enterprise software" look
- Dark blue "AI startup" with purple gradients
